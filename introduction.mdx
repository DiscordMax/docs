## Report Bot

Welcome to Report Bot, the perfect tool to empower your Discord communities. With Report Bot, you can easily report users, look up case histories, create report tickets, and access moderation commands—all in one place. It's simple to set up and designed to streamline moderation, ensuring your community remains safe and well-managed.
